# Chapter 1: Down the Pothole (Kindergarten)

First join to the first Board review. The First Morning (7 steps) and then 13 To-Do steps
(`Config.Tutorial`). Its finale is the Vex Prep Job: down the pothole, through the sewer, up into
Headmaster Grindle's office, knock him out, take his hoverboard, and steal a student unseen.

Story: you arrive on Otis's bus. Mr. Wobblesworth, who is retiring, hands you the keys to the empty
old school. Dr. Vex's limo glides past with Crumpet driving. Crumpet raids you; goons bounce off your
lasers. Skater Kid, your first transfer, is snatched to the Homework Factory, and you free him. Then:
- Hector's hall pass;
- the tiara chase;
- Crumpet's crew;
- Stan's sewer map (a Factory heist);
- scouting Vex Prep, and the pothole;
- the Job.

Hook out: Vex's "PRINCIPAAAAL!" over the PA, and the Board makes you an Elementary School.
